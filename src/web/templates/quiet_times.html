Quiet times

While a quiet time is running the device goes to sleep: no ringtone, no lights, and pressing any button just flashes all nine lights three times. Messages that arrive still get saved, and appear on the buttons as soon as the quiet time ends. A window may run past midnight — bedtime 19:00 to 07:00 works as you would expect.
